ATDA / RESEARCH INTEGRITY / 30 OCT 2026

Related work & prior art

A public, source-linked review of what existed before the current ATDA implementation, what the architecture proposes, and which claims remain unverified.

Critical conclusion

ATDA combines established techniques in a documented research architecture. This is not proof of first invention, non-infringement, field-certified privacy, successful chemical sensing or production-ready post-quantum cryptography. No exhaustive patent search or independent source-code similarity audit has been completed.

Three important distinctions

01 · PRIVACY

50 events ≠ 50 people

The local k=50 gate counts distinct interaction events, not individuals. The observable 49/50 release decision means the protocol has no whole-system pure ε-DP guarantee.

Mathematical limitations

02 · HARDWARE

Isolation must be proved

Data diodes and chemical detection at transit gates are prior art. ATDA proposes independently isolated data paths and a physical PII barrier, but no hardware validation exists yet.

Hardware evidence plan

PQC is a migration plan
